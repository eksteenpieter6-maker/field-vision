SOUND RADAR

Point the top of your phone like a torch. Sweep slowly around you. Loud or interesting directions build up dense particle clouds.

Hold the phone at arm's length with your body behind it: your body blocks sound from behind, which sharpens direction.

Firefox detected: works, but SONAR is best in Chrome or Samsung Internet. Firefox can play sound very quietly or through the earpiece while the mic is on.

Silent unless you switch on SONAR (short quiet chirps). If something looks wrong, tap DIAG, then COPY DIAG.
